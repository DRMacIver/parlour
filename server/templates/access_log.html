Moderators who looked

Moderators and admins read only what their own groups and lists let them, like everyone else. When one needs to read a private thread, space or conversation to deal with a report or a problem, they say why, everyone who can read it is told, and it is listed here for each of them. These are the times that touched what you could read, whether or not you still can.
